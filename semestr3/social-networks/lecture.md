### SF-Networks

- Scale-Free Networks
    - some nodes (= hubs) have extremely many connections to other nodes
	- most nodes have just a few connections to other nodes
	- resilience to random disturbance
	- vulnerability to attacks
	- -> new application areas (malware protection, medicine, business)
	- different than random graphs (normal distribution of the number of edges)
- Erdős number
- examples: social networks, biological networks, socio-technical networks
- mechanisms: growth, preferential attachment
- "the rich get richer" (new nodes prefer to attach to nodes that are already popular)
- reliability
	- random failures: 80 % of randomly selected nodes can fail without framenting the cluster
	- coordinated attacks: the elimination of 5--15 % of all the hubs can cause the entire system to collapse
